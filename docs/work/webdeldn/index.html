WEBdeLDN

WEBdeLDN is small (but very cool) monthly meetup organise in London, with a couple of friends. We don’t have a fixed topic: every month we choose one subject and we invite two speakers to share their experience and their knowledge about it. I have designed the logo, the Keynote template for the slides, and the Mailchimp template for the newsletters.
